the idea →

Plan the warehouse like a blueprint, not a construction log.

Warehouse-as-Code describes how a warehouse should be built and which rules it runs by – as version-controlled YAML, checked by JSON Schema. Like Terraform: the code describes a design, not a running system.

building blocks

Four sketches of one warehouse

Each repo answers one question. They change at different speeds and are reviewed by different people. A site manifest pins them together per facility.

OrderOrchestration-as-Code How does an order get worked through?

Split into sub-orders, completion rules, what happens on a shortage.

 MasterData-as-Code What do we stock?

Items, packaging levels, barcodes, lot and best-before tracking, stock owner.

 Allocation-as-Code Where do we look first – and where does new stock go?

Search sequence, FIFO/FEFO, putaway rules, owner zones, fixed bins.

 Topology-as-Code How is the warehouse laid out?

Racks, aisles, AutoStore cells, doors – and which movements are allowed.

ideas & open space

What is drawn, what is still blank

Modeled

  • Physical structure and movement rules
  • Order splitting and fulfillment
  • Item master data
  • Stock search and putaway

Not modeled yet

  • Inventory and stock state
  • Wave and batch planning

Out of scope on purpose

  • Task execution (runtime WMS/WES)
  • Slotting optimization
  • Labor and yard management

Structure vs. strategies. Racks change during a rebuild; search and split rules change every week. They live in separate folders.

“Can” vs. “may”. Lanes say which paths physically exist. Movement rules say which of them are allowed.

Checked before go-live. Every change is validated per repo and across repos – also once a day.
